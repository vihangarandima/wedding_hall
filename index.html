Good questions

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp. We usually reply within the hour.

How far ahead should we book?

Weekends in wedding season (December–April and August) fill 9–12 months ahead. Weekdays are often free with 2–3 months' notice.

Is your kitchen halal? Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Our main kitchen is halal and never handles pork. A separate pure-vegetarian kitchen serves Hindu and Buddhist menus.

Do you host events on Poya days?

We follow Poya-day regulations. Ceremonies without alcohol can go ahead. We'll guide you when you pick a date.

Can we bring our own decorator or photographer?

Photographers, yes, always. Outside decorators are welcome on the Grand package with a short site briefing.

What happens if it rains?

Every outdoor booking includes a covered backup space at no extra cost. We make the call with you by noon on the day.

How much is the advance, and is it refundable?

25% reserves your date. You can move it once, free of charge, up to 90 days before.
